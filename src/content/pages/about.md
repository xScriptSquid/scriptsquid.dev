## Hi, I'm Ray

I'm a systems and security engineer who got here the long way. I started on the
help desk and worked up to running infrastructure across 26 locations and
roughly 825 endpoints. I like seeing a problem all the way through: building the
system, automating the tedious parts, and then testing it hard enough to trust
it.

These days I'm focused on the security side of that work, incident
investigation, hardening, and wireless auditing, and I'm moving toward doing it
full time.
