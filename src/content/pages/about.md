## Hi, I'm Ray

I'm a systems and security engineer who got here the long way — starting on
the help desk and working up to running infrastructure across 26 locations and
roughly 825 endpoints. I like the whole arc of a problem: standing a system
up, automating the boring parts, and then poking at it to see where it breaks.

These days I'm focused on the security side of that work — incident
investigation, hardening, and wireless auditing.
